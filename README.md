# Dream Loft Design

Create a premium, conversion-focused HOMEPAGE MOCKUP for London Dreams Loft [londondreamsloft.co.uk], a London loft conversion / home improvement company.

This is a visual redesign exercise. Do NOT redesign the entire website or create lots of extra pages. The objective is to produce a homepage that makes the owner immediately think:

“This looks like the quality of our work.”

OVERALL CREATIVE DIRECTION

Think:

London architecture magazine + premium residential interiors + exceptional local craftsmanship.

The company has excellent project photography, including beautiful kitchens, extensions, bathrooms, bedrooms and finished living spaces.

The website should feel:

Premium

Warm

Architectural

Sophisticated

Trustworthy

Established

Human

Highly visual

It should NOT feel:

Like a generic builder website

Like a SaaS/startup website

Corporate

Overly luxurious or pretentious

Covered in icons, cards and gradients

Like a template

Let the photography do a lot of the selling.

COLOUR PALETTE

Use a restrained, editorial palette:

Warm ivory / off-white: #F5F1E8

Deep charcoal: #202321

Warm stone: #E5DED2

Muted sage: #737A68

Very subtle brass: #B39A68

The page should be predominantly warm ivory and charcoal, with stone used for secondary sections.

Avoid bright blue, bright orange, saturated green or excessive gold.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant editorial serif for major headlines, paired with a clean modern sans-serif for body copy and navigation.

Headings should feel confident and spacious.

Avoid overly decorative fonts.

HEADER

Create a clean, refined header.

Left: LONDON DREAM LOFTS

Navigation:

Loft Conversions

Extensions

Renovations

Our Work

About

Right: GET A FREE QUOTE

Keep the header visually light and premium.

On mobile, simplify appropriately.

HERO — THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTION

Use a large, stunning project photograph as the hero background or dominant visual.

The image should feel like a finished architectural/interior photography shoot.

Do not cover the photograph with lots of text.

Suggested hero copy:

Small eyebrow: LONDON LOFT & HOME TRANSFORMATION SPECIALISTS

Main headline:

Beautiful Spaces.
Thoughtfully Built.

Supporting text:

Loft conversions, extensions and renovations designed around the way you live.

Primary CTA:

GET A FREE QUOTE

Secondary understated link:

VIEW OUR WORK

The hero should feel spacious and confident.

Do NOT put seven trust points, paragraphs of copy or lots of badges in the hero.

Under or just below the CTA, include a very restrained trust line such as:

Experienced team · Quality craftsmanship · Fully managed projects

IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE HERO

Introduce the company with a short, elegant statement.

Headline:

More Than Extra Space.
A Better Home.

Short paragraph explaining that London Dream Lofts creates carefully considered loft conversions, extensions and renovations, managing the project from initial planning through to completion.

Keep this section visually clean.

TRUST STRIP

Create a sophisticated horizontal trust section rather than a huge collection of icons.

Potential points:

Experienced Team
Fully Managed Projects
Quality Craftsmanship
Clear Communication

If specific figures, guarantees, insurance levels or accreditations are confirmed from the existing business information, use them accurately. Do not invent statistics, awards or guarantees.

PROJECTS / OUR WORK

This should be one of the strongest sections on the page.

Headline:

Spaces We've Transformed

Use large editorial photography in an asymmetric gallery/grid.

Prioritise:

Beautiful kitchen/extension imagery

Wide interior views showing the transformation

Loft/bedroom spaces

High-quality bathrooms

Interesting architectural details

Do NOT make every image into a tiny equal-sized card.

Allow some photographs to be large and immersive.

Each project can have a simple label such as:

SIDE INFILL & EXTENSION
LOFT CONVERSION
FULL HOME RENOVATION

Keep text minimal.

IMPORTANT: The project photography seen on Google/reviews should only be used on the final website if London Dreams Loft provides/authorises the original images. For this mockup, use the available imagery where possible or clearly structure the design so their supplied project photography can be dropped in.

WHY LONDON DREAM LOFTS

Create a concise section communicating why homeowners choose them.

Headline:

One Team. One Clear Process.

Explain that clients don't have to coordinate multiple trades themselves.

Focus on:

Experienced project management

Quality workmanship

Clear communication

Attention to detail

A single accountable team

Avoid generic “We are the best” language.

SERVICES

Present three elegant visual categories:

LOFT CONVERSIONS
Create valuable new living space from your existing roof.

EXTENSIONS
Thoughtfully designed additional space that feels natural to your home.

RENOVATIONS
Beautifully finished improvements that transform how your home works.

Use photography rather than excessive icons.

REVIEWS / SOCIAL PROOF

The Google reviews appear to contain strong customer photography and positive homeowner feedback.

Create a beautiful testimonial section that makes reviews feel authentic rather than like generic marketing copy.

Use a heading such as:

Loved by Homeowners

Display 2–3 short genuine review excerpts if the existing reviews are available.

Include a subtle Google rating/review reference only if the exact rating and review count can be verified. Do not invent or exaggerate review statistics.

Where possible, visually connect testimonials to the actual finished projects.

FINAL CTA

End with a strong but elegant conversion section.

Headline:

Ready to Transform Your Home?

Supporting copy:

Tell us what you're thinking and we'll help you explore what's possible.

CTA:

GET A FREE QUOTE

Keep the section visually striking but uncluttered.

FOOTER

Clean, minimal footer with:

London Dream Lofts

Main services

Contact details

Service area

Privacy / terms

Strong final contact CTA

DESIGN RULES

Use generous whitespace.

Use large photography.

Use subtle borders rather than heavy shadows.

Use restrained corner rounding.

Use tasteful hover effects and very subtle animation only where it improves the experience.

Avoid:

Excessive cards

Excessive rounded pills

Generic construction icons

Stock photos of builders wearing hard hats

Gradients

Loud colours

Huge walls of text

Excessive animation

Overloaded navigation

Multiple competing CTAs

The finished result should feel like a high-end London residential brand, while remaining believable for an established local construction company.

Most importantly:

The website should sell the quality of the finished home, not simply list the services.

Make the photography, typography, whitespace and confidence of the design do the heavy lifting.

Build the homepage as a polished, realistic visual mockup suitable for showing directly to the business owner.
